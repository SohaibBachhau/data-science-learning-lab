## Three ideas easily confused

1. **Stationary** does not mean constant or necessarily uncorrelated.
2. **Causal ARMA** refers to a past-shock representation; **Granger causal** refers to prediction; **structural causation** refers to interventions.
3. **Uncorrelated** does not imply **independent**; **white noise** need not be IID.
