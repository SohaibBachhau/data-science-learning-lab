## What you should be able to do

- Compute and convert simple and log returns, including multi-period returns.
- Explain portfolio weighting and market capitalisation.
- Interpret mean, volatility, quantiles and frequency conversions.
- Explain volatility clustering and the different ACFs of returns and squared returns.
- Apply the Ljung-Box and Jarque-Bera decision rules.
- Interpret skewness, kurtosis, fat tails, Student-t degrees of freedom and QQ plots.
- Explain why correlations across assets may change with market conditions.
